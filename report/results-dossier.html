What it means

Retrieval is the decisive win. C beats B by +2.80 (p<0.001) — supplying the passages at inference is what lets the small model answer. This matches the thesis.

But fine-tuning also helped closed-book (B > A, +1.27, p=0.007) — which surprised me, since a grounded fine-tune is not usually expected to beat base. The component breakdown shows why: B's gain is concentrated in groundedness (0.18 → 0.87), not deep facts. The base model gives verbose, confident hallucinations (groundedness caps at 0 for invented details); the fine-tune learned a tighter, more-grounded answer shape.

So "fine-tuning teaches shape, not facts" still holds — the facts only arrive with retrieval (C's correctness 3.85 vs B's 2.35) — but that shape was enough to raise the score here more than it did in the reference.
